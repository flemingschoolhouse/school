Week 3 · Tongue Peek: Quiet TH
Nayeli

Tongue peek! Put the tip of your tongue just between your teeth — let it peek out a tiny bit, like a turtle peeking out of its shell. Now blow air: “thhh.” That's the quiet TH. Check in the mirror — can you see your tongue?

Mouth Gym warm-up (1 min): tongue elevator up & down ×5 · tongue peek (tip just between your teeth) ×5 · smile–kiss ×5
▶ Watch & copy

not “f” or “s”
no bunny teeth · no snake

Nayeli

Buzzy TH! Same tongue peek — but turn your voice ON so your tongue tickles: “thhh-uh.” Hand on your throat — feel the buzz? Lots of little words use buzzy TH: the, this, that.
